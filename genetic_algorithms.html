Concept Reference ▼
1. Population: Pool of valid TSP city permutations.
2. Fitness Function: Evaluates route quality; higher value indicates shorter route.
3. Selection: Chooses fit parent routes for producing next generation.
4. Crossover: Combines permutation segments from two parents without duplicating cities.
5. Mutation: Introduces variation by swapping or reversing city sub-sequences to prevent premature convergence.
6. Evaluation: Quantitative comparison of metrics across generations.
7. Termination: Triggered manually or when population diversity stagnates near 0%.
